About BioMind

A biology learning platform designed to feel like Khan Academy + Duolingo + Quizlet for biology.

The vision

BioMind is my attempt to build an A–Z biology platform that can take a student from middle school biology all the way through university‑level and MCAT‑level biology, using interactive tools instead of just static notes.

What BioMind teaches

The curriculum spans foundations (chemistry of life, cells, genetics, evolution), AP Biology, and university‑level topics like molecular biology, cell signaling, genetics & genomics, biochemistry, physiology, ecology, and evolution.

How it works

Under the hood, BioMind is built around a few core engines: an AI tutor interface, structured learning paths, a practice engine, and interactive tools. Content is stored in data files so the platform can keep growing without rewriting the core code.
